About Habesha Market

Habeshan Market is Ethiopian Marketplace created for learning web development. Our mission is to connect customers with Ethiopian products, including fresh foods, traditional spices, handmade crafts, and locally made products.

We are proud celebrating Ethiopian culutures by supporting local farmers, artisans, and small businesses while providing quality products to our community.
